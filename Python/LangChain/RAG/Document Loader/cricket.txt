On a field where silence hums before the storm,
Leather meets willow, a ritual reborn.
The bowler runs in like a question unasked,
The batter replies with a blade finely tasked.

The ball whispers secrets through air thick with fate,
A flick of the wrist decides glory or wait.
Crowds rise like waves in a stadium sea,
Each heartbeat syncing with what’s yet to be.

A cover drive paints poetry across the green,
A six writes thunder where calm had once been.
Fielders chase dreams that roll fast on the ground,
While time itself pauses at every appeal’s sound.

From dusty gullies to lights burning bright,
Cricket is more than a game, it’s a fight.
Not just of runs, but of patience and will,
Of silence before noise, of calm before thrill.

So when stumps are drawn and the echoes fade slow,
What lingers is more than the final score’s glow.
It’s the story of moments, stitched ball by ball,
A game, yet a universe, holding it all. 🏏✨

In the swing of the seam and the spin of the wrist,
Lie puzzles unsolved in the morning’s cool mist.
A yorker like lightning, a bouncer that roars,
Each delivery knocking on destiny’s doors.

The scoreboard may tick, but legends are made,
In grit under pressure, not just runs displayed.
A captain’s field set like pieces of art,
Each move a quiet gamble, each risk a bold start.

And somewhere a child with a bat carved from dream,
Replays these moments by a sunlit stream.
For cricket is timeless, it travels afar,
From backyard battles to the grand avatar.

So play on, O game of elegance and fire,
Of heartbreak, of hope, of endless desire.
For long after cheers dissolve into night,
Cricket still glows in memory’s light. 🌙🏏
